Recruitment Workflow Guide
  1. Navigate to Post Opening to view all active placement drives scheduled by the college.
  2. Select a drive and provide role details, package (LPA), minimum CGPA cutoff, and vacancies.
  3. Once the placement coordinator approves your opening, eligible students can submit applications.
  4. Review student resumes under Applications and move candidates through Shortlisted, Selected, or Rejected states.
